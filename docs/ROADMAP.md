# Urban Spoon — Roadmap

This document is the authoritative product phasing. The PRD describes the full product; [IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) breaks this roadmap into build milestones (M0–M12). "Phase" always refers to the phases below.

## MVP

Customer discovery, menu, cart, basic coupons (platform and restaurant codes with percentage or fixed value, minimum order, maximum discount, and usage limits), order intent and online payment, order lifecycle, restaurant operations, delivery dispatch with a partner PWA, admin operations, refunds, settlements and partner payouts, reviews, support, security, observability, and recovery readiness. Single city.

## Phase 2

Smart Portable Cart and group ordering (one shared mechanism), advanced promotions and campaigns, loyalty, membership, scheduled ordering, cash on delivery, native delivery partner app with background location, Smart Food Comparison, "Why This Restaurant?", and richer analytics.

## Phase 3

AI assistant, recommendations, meal planning, review analysis, menu assistance, and voice intent.

## Phase 4

Corporate accounts, office ordering, catering quotations, home-food providers, and tiffin subscriptions.

## Phase 5

Multi-city configuration, advanced personalization, fraud controls, POS/accounting integrations, and marketplace optimization.
