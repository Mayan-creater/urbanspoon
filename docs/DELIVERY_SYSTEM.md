# Urban Spoon — Delivery System

Delivery and assignment (offer) states are defined in [STATE_MACHINES.md](STATE_MACHINES.md). A delivery tracks the physical progress of one order; an assignment is one offer to one partner. A delivery may have many assignments over time, but at most one accepted assignment at once.

## Dispatch Inputs

The dispatcher considers partner verification, online status, one-open-offer-at-a-time, recent location, straight-line distance to the restaurant, delivery radius, prior offers for the delivery, and the restaurant's preparation estimate. Restaurant acceptance accepts a 5-120 minute prep estimate (20 minute default); offers begin at the ready-time lead window (10 minute default). Partner offer cards use Google Routes when `GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY` is configured; coordinates are sent to Google as approved for the Asia/Americas rollout. Calls time out after 2.5 seconds, then use optional OSRM (`DELIVERY_ROUTING_URL`) or straight-line estimates. Google's market coverage and terms must be verified for each launch country; provider failures degrade to the labeled straight-line estimate. Geographic delivery zones, vehicle compatibility, workload scoring, and fairness ranking remain follow-up work.

## Dispatch Algorithm

A delivery is created in `PENDING_ASSIGNMENT` when the restaurant accepts the order. A ten-second sweep starts due dispatches and expires old offers. The dispatcher locks the delivery and candidate partner rows, chooses the nearest eligible partner, and creates one time-limited offer. A database partial unique index and conditional writes prevent multiple accepted assignments for one delivery. A partner may have at most one outstanding offer. Rejection or expiry offers the next eligible partner; release before pickup returns the delivery to `PENDING_ASSIGNMENT`. Admins can manually force dispatch. Offer TTL defaults to 90 seconds and is configurable from 10 to 900 seconds; dispatch radius defaults to 50 km.

## Location and Evidence

Partners submit authenticated location updates while online or busy, rate-limited to one every five seconds with a serialized database check. A delivery ID is accepted only when assigned to that partner. An online partner without a location heartbeat for 90 seconds is taken offline and excluded from dispatch. Raw locations older than 30 days are deleted as new locations arrive. The six-digit delivery code is derived from a server secret, stored as a hash, shown to the owning customer through tracking, and required for completion. The production secret must be at least 32 characters.

Partners can upload identity/vehicle documents while pending review and pickup/drop-off evidence at the corresponding delivery step. Uploads accept PDF, JPEG, PNG, and WebP up to 10 MB, check file signatures, and are encrypted with AES-256-GCM in a private local directory; Postgres stores metadata and hashes only. The directory must be persistent and private, and `DELIVERY_EVIDENCE_ENCRYPTION_KEY` must be a separately managed base64 32-byte key. Manual approval requires both identity and vehicle documents; admins view them through authenticated, no-store responses. The PWA can download only its own evidence. Malware scanning and managed object-storage support are not included; do not expose this single-node local vault to production workloads without an encrypted persistent volume, backups, key rotation, scanning, and an approved retention policy.

For the MVP the delivery partner app is an installable Progressive Web App in the Next.js application. It sends geolocation only while the page is open in the foreground and does not cache authenticated API data offline. Customer tracking hides locations older than five minutes and returns no location when updates are unavailable. A native partner app with background location is planned for Phase 2.

## Earnings and Payouts

Delivery completion creates idempotent itemized earnings with a versioned calculation snapshot: fixed base pay, optional distance pay, optional completion incentive, and any cash tip the partner reports as already received. Development defaults are 500 minor units base, 100 minor units per straight-line kilometre, and zero completion incentive. Configure `DELIVERY_BASE_EARNING_MINOR`, `DELIVERY_DISTANCE_EARNING_MINOR_PER_KM`, and `DELIVERY_COMPLETION_INCENTIVE_MINOR`; production requires explicit values. A cash tip is marked already paid and excluded from payout batches.

Checkout offers no tip, 5%, 10%, 15%, and a custom amount. Percentage tips are calculated on the discounted item subtotal; custom tips are capped at 50% of that subtotal. The tip is persisted on the order intent/order and included in the payment-attempt amount. Automatic cancellation refunds return the entire captured amount, including the tip, so cancelled/refunded orders retain no tip. The current checkout pricing excludes tips from the configured tax base; local tax treatment must be confirmed before enabling each market. The current M5 payment adapter is test-only, so production card tips cannot be charged until a payment processor is selected and integrated.

Partners request idempotent payout batches over completed date ranges. Earnings are reserved into one request, admins may approve/reject it, and rejection releases earnings. Selected rails are Razorpay Route for India/INR and Stripe Connect for US/USD and the euro-area/EUR, subject to provider account approval and recipient eligibility. See [Razorpay Route eligibility](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/route/) and [Stripe cross-border payout availability](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/cross-border-payouts). China/CNY and Russia/RUB are recorded as markets but payout requests are disabled until a locally available, legally approved provider is selected. The provider is stored with each payout; actual connected-account onboarding and transfer execution still require provider credentials, recipient account linking, and live payment settlement. `DELIVERY_PAYOUT_MODE=sandbox` (the non-production default) settles workflow state without moving funds. Manual external-transfer reconciliation remains available.

## Verification, Routing, and Live Updates

Admins may record manual review or an external KYC provider name/reference. HyperVerge and Signzy are evaluation candidates only; no vendor has been selected or integrated. Until that selection, manual review uses encrypted uploaded evidence; external references store only the provider name/reference. Set `GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY` for managed routes. Google documents its [Routes API](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/routes); use an API key restricted to the Routes API and the server's outbound IP. Optional OSRM fallback is configured by `DELIVERY_ROUTING_URL`. Partner and customer SSE endpoints stream authorized snapshots every five seconds, with polling retained as fallback.

## Local Setup

Generate an encryption key outside source control with `openssl rand -base64 32`, then set `DELIVERY_EVIDENCE_ENCRYPTION_KEY` in the API environment. Keep `DELIVERY_EVIDENCE_DIR` on the existing persistent private volume; managed evidence storage is intentionally unchanged. Optionally set `GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY` and `DELIVERY_ROUTING_URL`. For local workflow testing, use `DELIVERY_PAYOUT_MODE=sandbox`; this mode is rejected in production and never transfers funds. Run the API migrations before starting the services, then use `pnpm --filter @urbanspoon/web test:e2e` for the mock-backed desktop/mobile browser journeys.

## Acceptance

No delivery is offered to an unavailable or unverified partner, duplicate acceptance is rejected transactionally, reassignment and lifecycle changes are auditable, and customers receive degraded but truthful status when location updates fail. Local/test workflows cover manual evidence review, encrypted proof upload/download, Google Routes/OSRM/fallback estimates, itemized earnings, cash and digital tips, market-specific payout selection, sandbox payout reconciliation, and browser flows. Production still needs a selected/contracted KYC provider, managed evidence storage and scanning (deferred by request), an approved retention/key-rotation policy, Google coverage/billing verification for every country, connected payout accounts and live Razorpay/Stripe transfer execution, local payout rails for China/Russia, production card payments, and market-specific tip tax approval.
